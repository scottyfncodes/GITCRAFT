How the scores work

Each department's 0–100 score is its own measured result under a published rubric. The overall score is the weighted mean of the evaluated departments only (method gitcraft-score/2); departments that apply but have no measured result are Not Evaluated, carry no weight and are never filled in. Coverage shows how much of the applicable weight was evaluated. Release gates and approvals are separate pass/fail states. A previous score is compared only when the rubric and the department's own method are unchanged.
